# World War II in 60 Seconds

In 1939, the deadliest conflict in human history began when Germany invaded Poland.

Within months, Europe was split between the Axis powers and the Allied nations fighting for survival.

By 1941, the war went global as the Soviet Union and the United States entered the fight.

Over six brutal years, more than 70 million people lost their lives across land, sea, and air.

In 1945, the Allies finally prevailed, reshaping the modern world and creating the United Nations to prevent another global war.
